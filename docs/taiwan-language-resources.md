# Taiwan Mandarin and regional pronunciation resources

The Chinese Learning Hub focuses on Taiwan Traditional Chinese and Taiwan Mandarin. Taiwanese Hokkien and Hakka are outside this product's scope and are not developed as lessons, speech-recognition targets, pronunciation modules, dialect catalogs or corpus integrations.

## In-scope varieties

- Taiwan Mandarin (general): pronunciation, Zhuyin, tones, vocabulary and natural conversation.
- Taiwan Mandarin regional variation: optional advanced comparison of pronunciation, intonation and vocabulary based on documented linguistic research and properly authorized speech samples. Avoid stereotypes and do not infer an individual's region from their voice.

## Reviewed sources

- Mozilla Common Voice 27.0 Chinese (Taiwan): CC0-1.0 dataset card; do not re-host or re-share the dataset, and do not attempt speaker identification. https://mozilladatacollective.com/datasets/cmu5wkw5i00c6o107jnm3t51u
- Academia Sinica Taiwan Mandarin Sociophonetic Corpus: research corpus covering multiple regions; access and reuse terms must be checked, especially because interview metadata can be personal data. https://tmc.ling.sinica.edu.tw/corpus_list_ch/

No third-party audio dataset is copied into this repository. The site stores source metadata and links only. Dataset availability does not imply permission for model training, redistribution or commercial use.
